Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Freelancer Automation Suite? +
It's a collection of 10 AI-powered automation skills that handle common freelance tasks like GitHub issue management, web research, design work, and deployment—all from your terminal.
Do I need coding experience to use it? +
Basic terminal familiarity helps, but our natural language interface makes it accessible to non-technical users. Each skill can be triggered with simple commands.
Can I use only some skills and not others? +
Absolutely. You can enable/disable individual skills in the dashboard and only pay for what you use. Mix and match based on your workflow.
How do I cancel my subscription? +
Cancel anytime from your account settings. Your access continues until the end of your billing period. We also offer a 30-day money-back guarantee.
Is my data secure? +
Yes. We use industry-standard encryption, never sell your data, and all API communications are secure. You retain full ownership of all outputs.
Can I self-host the suite? +
Currently the suite runs as a cloud service, but self-hosted enterprise options are available for teams with specific compliance requirements. Contact us for details.
